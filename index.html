About

VSD Productivity integrates with Google Tasks to help users manage their personal tasks through a desktop interface.

Privacy Policy

VSD Productivity accesses Google Tasks data solely to provide task management functionality, including viewing, creating, updating, and deleting tasks.

Google OAuth credentials and authentication tokens are stored locally on the user's device.

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising.

Users may revoke the application's access through their Google Account security settings.
